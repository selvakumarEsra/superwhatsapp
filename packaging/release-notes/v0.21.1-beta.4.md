ZapFast 0.21.1-beta.4 (Znga) lets you copy messages kept under Deleted & edited and shows exactly what each edit changed. It also fixes the profile picture viewer and names the sender in group notifications.

## New

- **Copy kept messages.** Every entry under Insights → Deleted & edited has a **Copy** button, and its text can be selected with the mouse and copied with ⌘C / Ctrl+C. Copy takes the whole message, or an attachment's caption. The entry now shows the whole message, not just its first line.
- **See what each edit changed.** A "Before edit" entry highlights what that edit changed, word by word: removed words in red and struck through, added words in green. Each version is compared with the one that replaced it.

## Fixed

- **Profile pictures closed straight away.** Clicking the photo in a chat's info panel now opens it in the image viewer and keeps it open.
- **Group notifications are titled with the sender.** A group notification reads "Group · Sender" instead of naming your own account.

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- If an earlier version of an edited message was forgotten, the version before it shows without highlights.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
