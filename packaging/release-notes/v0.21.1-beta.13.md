SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.13 fixes pictures and logging in the new interface. It updates in place: your account, chats, settings and Znga stay as they are.

## Fixed

- **Pictures show again on macOS and Linux.** Profile pictures of contacts and groups, photos, video thumbnails and stickers stayed empty in 0.21.1-beta.12. They now show in the chat list and in chats.
- **Video in calls.** The other person's picture during a video call shows again on macOS and Linux.
- **The log is written again.** SuperWhatsapp writes its log file again, the one Settings › Files opens, so a problem can be reported with what happened.
- **A clear message instead of a crash** if moving your data from the old ZapFast folders fails at start. Nothing new is created, so the next start tries again.

## Known limitations

- On Linux, SuperWhatsapp needs WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0` on Debian and Ubuntu, `webkit2gtk4.1` on Fedora).
- On Windows, SuperWhatsapp uses Microsoft Edge WebView2. If the app does not open, install the WebView2 Runtime from Microsoft.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
