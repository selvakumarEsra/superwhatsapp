ZapFast 0.21.1-beta.6 (Znga) lets you pin as many chats as you like and retires mini-apps.

## New

- **Pin any number of chats.** WhatsApp keeps three pinned chats (twenty with WhatsApp Plus), and your phone unpins an older chat when another device pins one more. Your first pins still reach the phone as before; each pin beyond WhatsApp's limit is kept on this computer only, so your phone's pins are never pushed out. ZapFast says "Pinned on this computer only" when that happens. Unpinning works the same for every chat.

## Removed

- **Mini-apps, including Goal of the day.** They are no longer in the app. Their data in `miniapps.db` stays on your computer untouched; 0.21.1-beta.5 still has them.

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- A new interface (Tauri) is in progress and is not part of this release.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
