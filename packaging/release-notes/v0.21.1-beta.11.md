SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.11 is the first release with the new interface, built with Tauri and Solid.js. It replaces the previous one and updates in place: your account, chats, settings and Znga stay as they are.

## New in SuperWhatsapp

- **A new interface.** Every screen keeps its familiar layout, now drawn by your system's web view: the chat list, chats, Starred, Archived, Locked chats, Calls, contact details and the whole of Settings.
- **Calls.** Start a voice or video call from a chat's header or the Calls list. The call screen has Mute, the camera, a device picker and Hang up, and you can step back to your chats while the call goes on.
- **Contact details.** Add a number to your contacts or rename it right in the panel, copy the number, and see the latest photos and videos you shared.
- **Settings › Files.** See where your archive, downloads and log are, change the downloads folder, and see and clear what downloaded media takes up.
- **Znga reads web pages.** Paste a link in the Znga chat and ask about it. Znga reads the page and a few linked pages on the same site, and answers with links you can open. It only reads: it never fills in forms, signs in or buys anything.

## Known limitations

- On Linux, SuperWhatsapp now needs WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0` on Debian and Ubuntu, `webkit2gtk4.1` on Fedora). Without an app indicator library there is no tray icon.
- On Windows, SuperWhatsapp uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10 already have. If the app does not open, install the WebView2 Runtime from Microsoft.
- Video calls show the other person's picture, not their live video, for now.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
