SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.9 brings Znga into the new interface (SuperWhatsapp Preview) as a chat member and a proactive companion. The current SuperWhatsapp app is unchanged.

## New in SuperWhatsapp Preview

- **Znga is a chat.** Turn Znga on in Settings › Znga and a chat called Znga appears at the top of your chat list. Ask it about your chats, or tell it what to prepare: "tell Ada I'll call at 5", "remind me to pay Sam on Friday", "summarise the school group". The chat stays on this computer, encrypted, and is never sent to WhatsApp.
- **Znga drafts, you send.** Anything meant for someone else arrives as a draft with Send, Edit in chat and Discard. Nothing is sent until you press Send. When a name could mean two people, Znga asks which one.
- **Znga speaks first.** It tells you at once when a message needs you, briefs you when you come back, and reminds you of promises, upcoming plans and friends you have not talked to in a while. You can add a morning and an evening digest and set quiet hours. Each kind has its own switch.
- **Znga in every chat.** The ✨ button in the message field suggests a reply, catches you up, finds plans, and rewrites or translates what you typed. Your text changes only when you choose Use, and Undo puts it back.
- **Settings › Znga.** The engine and its key, Twin and excluded chats, what Znga does on its own, what it remembers about people, open promises, and the Sentinel's log of everything Znga did. Settings can now be searched.

## Known limitations

- Znga never reads locked or excluded chats, and plain-word instructions are recognised from their wording ("tell", "remind me", "summarise", "done"); anything else is answered as a question.
- The preview still lacks message actions, attachments, emoji, voice notes, media viewing, Insights and calls. Use SuperWhatsapp for these.
- SuperWhatsapp and SuperWhatsapp Preview use the same account and never run together.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
