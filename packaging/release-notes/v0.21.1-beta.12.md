SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.12 is the first release with the new interface, built with Tauri and Solid.js. It replaces the previous one and updates in place: your account, chats, settings and Znga stay as they are.

## New in SuperWhatsapp

- **Znga answers link to your messages.** When Znga answers from your chats, each message it used is cited by number and listed under the answer. Click one to open the chat at that message. Chat and group names in the answer open the chat too. Ask "show my messages that gave an email address" and you get one link per message.
- **Easier to read answers.** Znga's answers now use short headings, lists and bold for the key fact. Point at an answer for **Copy**, **Reply** (ask a follow-up about that answer) and **Forward** (puts the answer in another chat's message box; you send it).
- **Translate a message.** Point at a message in another language and click the translate button. Znga shows the translation under the message. Nothing in the chat changes and nothing is sent.
- **Znga gets to know people.** If you turn it on in Settings › Znga › People, Znga keeps a private profile of each contact and group (how you talk with them, topics, dates) and uses it for better drafts and answers. Sensitive details wait for your confirmation, and you can see, correct or delete everything Znga knows from a chat's info panel.
- **A new name for the app's folders.** Your settings, archive and logs move from the old ZapFast folders to SuperWhatsapp ones the first time you start this release, with their keys. Nothing to do on your side.
- **A new interface.** Every screen keeps its familiar layout, now drawn by your system's web view: the chat list, chats, Starred, Archived, Locked chats, Calls, contact details and the whole of Settings.
- **Calls.** Start a voice or video call from a chat's header or the Calls list. The call screen has Mute, the camera, a device picker and Hang up, and you can step back to your chats while the call goes on.
- **Contact details.** Add a number to your contacts or rename it right in the panel, copy the number, and see the latest photos and videos you shared.
- **Settings › Files.** See where your archive, downloads and log are, change the downloads folder, and see and clear what downloaded media takes up.
- **Znga reads web pages.** Paste a link in the Znga chat and ask about it. Znga reads the page and a few linked pages on the same site, and answers with links you can open. It only reads: it never fills in forms, signs in or buys anything.

## Known limitations

- On Linux, SuperWhatsapp now needs WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0` on Debian and Ubuntu, `webkit2gtk4.1` on Fedora). Without an app indicator library there is no tray icon.
- On Windows, SuperWhatsapp uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10 already have. If the app does not open, install the WebView2 Runtime from Microsoft.
- Video calls show the other person's picture, not their live video, for now.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
