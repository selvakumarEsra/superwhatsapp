SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.10 brings Znga into SuperWhatsapp itself as a chat. The separate Znga window is gone.

## New in SuperWhatsapp

- **Znga is a chat.** Turn Znga on in Settings › Znga and a chat called Znga appears at the top of your chat list. The ✨ button in the sidebar opens it too. Ask it about your chats, or tell it what to prepare: "tell Ada I'll call at 5", "remind me to pay Sam on Friday", "summarise the school group". It has the same message field as your other chats. The chat stays on this computer and is never sent to WhatsApp.
- **Znga drafts, you send.** Anything meant for someone else arrives as a card with Send, Edit in chat and Discard. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Znga speaks first.** It tells you when a message needs you and reminds you of promises and plans. You can add a morning and an evening digest and set quiet hours in Settings › Znga.
- **Znga in every chat.** The ✨ items in the + menu of the message field suggest a reply, catch you up, find plans, and rewrite or translate what you typed. Your text changes only when you choose Use, and Undo puts it back.
- **Settings › Znga.** The engine and its key, what Znga does on its own, open promises, and what it remembers about people now live in Settings. Search Settings for "Znga" or "engine" to jump there.

## Known limitations

- Znga never reads locked or excluded chats. Plain-word instructions are recognised from their wording ("tell", "remind me", "summarise", "done"); anything else is answered as a question.
- Znga's chat takes text only: no attachments, stickers or voice messages.
- SuperWhatsapp and SuperWhatsapp Preview use the same account and never run together.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
