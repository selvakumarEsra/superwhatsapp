ZapFast 0.21.1-beta.5 (Znga) makes Znga a fuller companion: it remembers across restarts, tracks promises, knows the people you talk to, and answers from your whole archive. It also brings mini-apps, starting with Goal of the day, and a Favourites tab when forwarding.

## New

- **Goal of the day.** A first mini-app: open the target button on the left rail and add it to your own "Message yourself" chat. Each morning (08:00) Znga proposes one goal with up to three steps, drawn from yesterday's unfinished goal, promises due soon and today's plans. It checks in at 13:00 and recaps at 20:00 with your streak. Reply `/goal ok`, `/goal done`, `50%`, `step 2`, `/goal carry`, `/goal pause` or `/goal help`, or just tell it how it's going. Tick steps, set your own goal, change the times and days, or delete the history from its card. At most three scheduled messages a day, and never to anyone but you.
- **Mini-apps.** Small apps that live in one chat. You see what each one may do before adding it, every action passes Znga's Sentinel and is logged, and what an app keeps is encrypted on this computer and deleted when you remove it.
- **Favourites when forwarding.** The Forward dialog has **All** and **Favourites** tabs; Favourites lists your favourite contacts and groups in your phone's order. Search and picks carry across tabs.
- **Znga remembers.** The Ask thread, facts about people, promises and the audit log survive restarts, encrypted in `znga.db`. On start, Znga picks up from your recent messages instead of starting empty.
- **Promises.** Znga notices what you promised ("I'll send the photos Friday") and what was asked of you, reminds you before it's due, and offers a draft. Mark them done or dismiss them.
- **People.** Short facts about each contact (birthdays, family names, what they're going through), which you can see, add and delete. They help the twin's drafts and check-ins.
- **Ask your whole archive.** Ask and your own chat search every message on this computer, not just recent ones, and say what they found.
- **Catch up, calendar, voice notes.** Catch up on the open chat with what changed and a suggested reply; turn plans in a chat into calendar files; summarise long voice notes (with a transcription server you choose, local by default).
- **Relationship coach.** For your close ones: how your rhythm changed and an opener in your voice.
- **Per-account persona.** Each linked account can have its own name, tone and briefing setting.
- **Explain an edit.** Under Insights → Deleted & edited, Explain says in a sentence what an edit changed.
- **Faster, stoppable answers.** Answers appear as they're written, never wait behind background work, and can be stopped.
- **The Sentinel.** Every Znga action passes one gate and is logged (readable in Setup). Text other people wrote can inform an answer but never trigger an action.

## Fixed

- **Deleted & edited loads quickly** even with many kept copies.

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- Mini-apps are built in; Goal of the day is the only one so far and can be added only to your own chat.
- Voice-note summaries need a transcription server (OpenAI-compatible); none is bundled.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
