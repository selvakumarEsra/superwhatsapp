SuperWhatsapp 0.21.1-beta.15 makes Znga's answers something you can follow and act on, and translates messages in place.

## What changes

- **Znga's answers link to your messages.** Ask Znga something like "show my messages that have an email address" and every message it mentions is a link naming the chat and the date. Clicking it opens that chat at that message and highlights it, loading older history when needed. Answers that cite messages end with a Sources list. The links are kept with the answer, so they still work after a restart.
- **Easier to read.** Znga's answers are laid out with headings, lists and bold text instead of plain paragraphs.
- **Copy, Reply and Forward on Znga's answers.** Hover an answer to copy it as plain text, reply to it (your next question is asked about that answer), or forward it: the chat you pick opens with the text ready above its composer, and nothing is sent until you send it.
- **Translate messages in place.** Hover a message written in another language, in a one-to-one or group chat, and choose **Translate to English** beside React, Reply and Forward. The bubble shows Znga's translation, marked as translated, and the button becomes **Show original**. The message menu has the same toggle, and a bar above the composer offers to translate the recent ones at once. Only the message you translate is sent to Znga's engine.

## Known limitations

- Links and translations need Znga to be on. Locked chats and chats excluded from Znga never show them.
- Which messages count as another language is decided on your computer from their letters and common words. Very short messages are not offered.
- The macOS bundle ID stays `me.paolino.fastsapp` for this release so it updates in place.
- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time, and allow keychain access when asked.
