## What is SuperWhatsapp?

A desktop app for WhatsApp on macOS, Windows and Linux with Znga, an AI agent
that tells you which messages need you, briefs you on what you missed and
drafts replies in your voice. See [the overview](/what-is-superwhatsapp/).

## Do I still need my phone?

You link SuperWhatsapp once by scanning a code with WhatsApp on your phone,
as you would for WhatsApp's own desktop app. After that it keeps working on
its own, even when your phone is off. See
[Getting started](/getting-started/#link-with-your-phone).

## How much does it cost?

SuperWhatsapp is free during the beta. Znga can use a free local model, or
an AI service you already pay for (Claude, Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible
service), in which case that service bills you for what Znga uses.

## Will Znga send messages for me?

No. Znga drafts; you send. Every message to someone else waits for your
click. WhatsApp bans accounts for automated messaging, and the people you
talk to should know a message from you came from you.

## Does the AI read all my chats?

Only after you turn Znga on, and only the chats you allow. Locked chats and
chats you exclude are never read. Cloud engines ask for your consent first.
Choose a local model and nothing leaves your computer. See
[Privacy and security](/privacy/).

## Which AI can I use?

Claude through Claude Code or an API key, Gemini, any OpenAI-compatible
service, or a local model with Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp. See
[Set up Znga](/znga/#set-up-znga).

## Can I use several WhatsApp numbers?

Yes. Link more than one number and switch between them in one window, each
with its own Znga persona if you like.

## Is it safe for my account?

SuperWhatsapp is unofficial. It behaves like a normal linked device and never
automates sending, but WhatsApp may restrict accounts that use unofficial
clients. Read [Account safety](/privacy/#account-safety) before you start.

## Is SuperWhatsapp made by WhatsApp?

No. SuperWhatsapp is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by
or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp is a trademark
of WhatsApp LLC.

## Where do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

On [GitHub issues](https://github.com/selvakumarEsra/superwhatsapp/issues).
Include what happened, what you expected and roughly when, and attach the log
described in [Settings and files](/settings-and-files/).
